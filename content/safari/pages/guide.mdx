> Run a safari agency with friends in chaotic 2–4 player co-op. Drive a fully destructible, physics-based truck, take care of tourists, and survive wildlife encounters. Film the chaos, watch your videos together, and grow your in-game channel.

## Early Access, in the developers’ answers

The store page is labeled coming soon to Early Access. The developer notes on that page, read 7 November 2026, are:

- **Why Early Access?** “Safari-TV is built around unpredictable moments with friends. Early Access helps us improve the game based on real player feedback and make the chaos even more fun.”
- **How long?** “We're aiming for around 6 months, but this may change depending on development and community feedback.”
- **How the full version should differ.** “We plan to add more locations, wildlife, upgrades, and gameplay variety, while improving performance, multiplayer, and overall polish.”
- **Current state.** “The core co-op experience is playable: explore the safari, film wildlife, transport tourists, upgrade your equipment, and cause chaos with friends. Expect some bugs and rough edges.”
- **Price during and after Early Access.** “We may increase the price as we add more content, but no final decision has been made.”
- **Community.** “We're active on Discord and Steam, listening to feedback, fixing bugs, and using player suggestions to help shape future updates.”

“Upgrade your equipment” is the only upgrade sentence in that current-state answer. It does not name a part, a cost, or a menu. The full-version answer says more upgrades are planned, which means the launch list is not the last list. See [Truck](/truck).

“Film wildlife” and “more … wildlife” are the wildlife sentences. The screenshots show particular animals. They are not a field guide. See [Wildlife](/wildlife).

## Playtests, wishlists, and keys

These numbers are the announcements’ own claims. This site did not read a live wishlist counter.

- **27 Aug 2026**, by zuflucht34, signed Alex and Dima. The post says Safari-TV reached 25,000 wishlists in three days.
- **27 Aug 2026**, the same day, also by zuflucht34. Closed playtest sign-ups for the first 1,000 players, via “Request Access” on the Steam page.
- **13 Sep 2026**, by zuflucht34, signed Alex and Dima. The post says the game passed 70,000 wishlists.
- **20 Oct 2026**, by alexander.gamedev34. The post says playtest access had been granted to 20,000 players, that in-game recordings could be saved to a PC, and that a two-day video contest was open. Prizes in that post: 1st place, 4 Safari-TV keys plus a $50 Steam gift card; 2nd and 3rd, Safari-TV keys; a separate $200 Steam gift card if a video passed 1 million views. Links went in the Discord `#gallery` channel. Views were counted on the social posts, not inside the game.
- **22 Oct 2026**, by alexander.gamedev34. The post says Safari-TV reached 100,000 wishlists.
- **28 Oct 2026**, by alexander.gamedev34. The playtest build was available to streamers and influencers. People without access were told to open a Discord ticket and request keys, and that keys at that stage were only for streamers and influencers. The post says everyone else would see the game at launch in two weeks.

The parent app’s `dlc` array contains **5164540**. That id’s own appdetails record is type `game`, name **Safari-TV Playtest**, `coming_soon` false, release date 28 Oct 2026, developers Mercy Games and stre1itzia, no publisher field, categories Multi-player, Co-op, and Online Co-op, `is_free` false, and an empty package list. The short description was empty. This page reports that API shape. It does not relabel the playtest app as a soundtrack or a paid add-on.
