The truck is the thing the store puts in the second sentence and the second heading. This page collects those lines and the frames that show the vehicle. It does not invent a handling model, a durability number, or an upgrade tree.

## The two official sentences

The short description says you “drive a fully destructible, physics-based truck.” The About This Game heading says “Drive a physics-based safari truck.” Both are on the Steam page for app 5118500. “Fully destructible” appears in the short description and in the page’s meta description. It does not appear as a stat block.

Driving is also one of the popular user-defined tags on that page (Driving, Physics). Tags are not the developers’ feature list. The sentences above are.

## Equipment upgrades

The Early Access “current state” answer says the playable core includes “upgrade your equipment.” The “full version” answer says the plan is to add “more locations, wildlife, upgrades, and gameplay variety.” Read together, equipment upgrades exist in the build they are describing, and more upgrades are planned after Early Access. The notes do not name:

- a part, tool, or attachment
- a currency or a cost
- a menu, a level, or a branch
- whether an upgrade is shared by the crew or owned by one player

<Callout kind="warn" title="No upgrade table">
A crate of tools, a loose tire, or a missing panel in a screenshot is a picture. The store page does not caption any of those frames as the upgrade screen. This page will not turn them into a shopping list.
</Callout>

Until a patch note or the client prints the list, the only safe wording is the developers’ own: you upgrade equipment, and more upgrades are planned. The [guide](/guide) quotes the Early Access answers in full.

The wide shot is a yellow open-sided truck with a canopy, passengers along the side benches, a large grey animal beside the cab, and a character in the foreground holding a camera.

## The PC the store asks for

The truck page is not the spec page, but the requirements are the only hardware numbers Steam printed. Minimum graphics are a GTX 1060 or RX 6600 XT. Recommended graphics are an RTX 2060 or RX 7600xt or equivalent. Both blocks ask for DirectX 11, broadband, and 5 GB. Minimum OS is Windows 10. Recommended OS is Windows 11. macOS and Linux are false, and those requirement lists were empty. The odd “1 MB RAM” line is discussed on the [guide](/guide). It is quoted there so it is not quietly corrected into a guess.
