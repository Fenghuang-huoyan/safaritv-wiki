Safari-TV’s store page promises wildlife and then shows it in screenshots. It does not publish a bestiary. This page lists the sentences, then the animals that are visible in the official frames checked on 7 November 2026. If a species is missing here, the store text did not name it and the screenshots did not show it.

## The sentences

The store puts the wildlife claim in four places:

- About heading: “Take care of your tourists and survive unexpected wildlife encounters.”
- Short description: “survive wildlife encounters.”
- Early Access current state: “film wildlife.”
- Early Access full-version plan: “more … wildlife.”

“Unexpected” is the store’s adjective. The page does not define a trigger, a distance, or a fail state. “Film wildlife” is the verb in the current-state answer, next to exploring, transporting tourists, upgrading equipment, and causing chaos. Filming is also its own About heading: post videos to gain in-game views and subscribers. The views and subscribers are described as in-game. No target number is printed.

The full-version sentence means the Early Access animal set is not presented as the final set. A species in a launch screenshot can still be joined by others. This page will not guess which ones.

<Callout kind="note" title="Emoji are not a roster">
The 22 October 2026 wishlist post uses a crocodile emoji. The 13 September and 27 August posts use a rhinoceros emoji, and the playtest sign-up post uses a lion emoji. The posts do not say those animals are in the build. The lion also appears in a screenshot, which is a different kind of evidence and is listed below. The crocodile does not appear in the screenshots checked here.
</Callout>

The same September and August posts say “70,000 tourists” and “25,000 tourists” are waiting. Both posts are wishlist milestones, signed as thank-you notes. They are not an in-game passenger count.

## Frames from the store

Giraffes at a waterhole, with two small characters in the grass in front of them. The store does not caption the shot with a zone name.

![Giraffes at a waterhole in an official Safari-TV screenshot](/images/official/steam/ss-09.jpg)

A hippo in tall grass, mouth open, next to a blue portable toilet and a character. The toilet is in the frame. The store text does not explain it.

![A hippo beside a blue portable toilet](/images/official/steam/ss-05.jpg)

A lion beside the yellow truck, inside a viewfinder overlay that includes a red REC marker and a battery icon. A character stands to the right. REC is the filming UI the store is showing. It is not a damage meter.

![A lion beside the truck inside a REC viewfinder](/images/official/steam/ss-10.jpg)

A large horned animal under trees, also inside a REC viewfinder, with characters close to it. The store does not print the species name on the page, so this guide does not assign one beyond what the picture shows: a large dark animal with a horn, in a dry woodland.

![A large horned animal under trees, filmed through the REC overlay](/images/official/steam/ss-02.jpg)

A fifth frame puts a large grey animal beside the truck cab while passengers sit in the open bed and someone holds a camera. It is on the [truck](/truck) page. The library hero also shows a giraffe behind the canopy of the same truck. The library capsule and header crop that scene under the Safari TV logo.

Animals is one of the user-defined tags on the store page. It matches the screenshots. It still is not a species list.

## Tourists sit next to the animals, and they are not the animals

The heading that mentions wildlife also says “take care of your tourists.” The Early Access line says “transport tourists.” Passengers are visible in the truck shots: stylized characters on the benches, in the cab, and gathered at a wooden camp. The store does not print a tourist type, a mood meter, or a payment. Taking care of them and transporting them are the verbs that were published. The [co-op](/coop) page keeps those verbs with the 2–4 player wording.

<Callout kind="warn" title="No danger ratings">
Nothing on the store page ranks the giraffe, the hippo, the lion, or the horned animal. A close camera and a REC marker do not mean a species is safe, and a hippo with its mouth open does not come with a damage figure. There is no tier list on this site.
</Callout>
